# Paktly brand mark

The Paktly mark represents three independent paths becoming one shared pact. Its open center keeps the symbol human and collaborative, while the extended mint path gives the silhouette a subtle lowercase `p` without relying on a literal monogram.

Production assets use deterministic flat vector geometry:

- Forest `#214C3A` — trust and financial seriousness
- Mint `#BFF1D3` — progress and shared possibility
- Coral `#FF816F` — people and lived experiences
- Cream `#F8F5ED` — the primary app-icon field

`paktly-mark.svg` is the canonical standalone mark. `paktly-app-icon.svg` is the canonical icon source. Keep clear space around the mark, do not rotate it, and do not introduce gradients, shadows, outlines, or alternate segment ordering.

`paktly-mark-exploration.png` records the AI-generated concept exploration that informed the final geometry. It is not a production asset and should not be referenced by the app or website.
